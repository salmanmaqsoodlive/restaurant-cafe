Our Story

Born From a
Passion for Coffee

The Golden Brew started in 2018 as a small corner café with a big dream: to create a space where exceptional food and coffee meet genuine warmth.

Today, we're Brisbane's favourite neighbourhood café — but we still do everything the same way we always have. Fresh ingredients. Expert baristas. And a welcome smile every single morning.
